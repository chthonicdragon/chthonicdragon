## Describing AI-assisted development

Allowed: "I build and ship products through AI-assisted development", "I coordinate AI coding agents, define requirements, evaluate implementations, and manage release readiness."
Forbidden: "I personally engineered every layer from scratch", "I can independently write and debug all production code", any wording that makes the agent invisible or the owner the sole author.

## Experience, interest, hypothesis, plan

Label which one a statement is. Practice = shipped, evidenced. Interest = what I study or want to learn. Hypothesis = a stated guess, marked as such. Plan = future, never past tense. Rejuv items use Implemented / In progress / Planned.

## Machine consciousness

Framed as an open scientific and philosophical question. Allowed: "open question", "uncertainty", "worth rigorous investigation". Forbidden: claiming current models are conscious, claiming proof, linking quantum physics to machine consciousness, using spiritual practice or transhumanist sympathy as evidence. No invented statistics ("only N% of people understand AI") and no claims of exceptionality.

## English and scannability

Natural, professional English. Short sentences, one idea per paragraph, concrete nouns. No em-dash chains. Hero stays short; the 120–200 word Personal Vision lives in Research. Do not publish an English proficiency level.

## Banned phrases

passionate AI enthusiast · visionary · disruptor · revolutionizing the future · cutting-edge · "40+ routes" / "220+ PRs" without a fresh measurement · "no degree" / "no education".
